Uplift Modeling - Project Notes
================================
Method: CausalML BaseTRegressor (T-learner) with LightGBM.
Dataset: Simulated treatment/control experiment based on the IBM Telco churn dataset.
Outcome: SimulatedChurnOutcome.
Important limitation: Treatment outcomes are synthetic and do not represent actual retention campaign results.
Estimated treatment effects are exploratory and should not be interpreted as validated causal business impact.
